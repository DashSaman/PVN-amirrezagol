# 063 — GRE — v1 Research Decision

Status: **`V1-HANDOFF-READY / NOT IMPLEMENTED`**.

Classification: generic routing encapsulation; not encrypted by itself.

Decision: **`ADVANCED SITE-TO-SITE / OS-KERNEL FIRST / LOW CONSUMER PRIORITY`**.

Prefer mature OS/kernel tunnel implementation and a typed privileged helper. Do not implement GRE packet encapsulation inside the UI.

Later v2 adds implementations/install matrix, protocol/wire flow, admin menus and deployment topologies.
